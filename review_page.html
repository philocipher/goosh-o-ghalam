Connecting…

Each recording's progress and edits are kept in the program's transcripts folder; opening one again carries on where it was left.

Choose what to listen to. A microphone hears you or the room; What plays on… hears whatever the computer plays through that sound card (a video call, a live stream, a video in the browser).

The transcript then appears like any recording's, a line at a time as it is said, and you can review it while it listens: unsure words are highlighted, click a word to correct it, and the other models' guesses follow a little later. Clips aren't played by themselves while listening (press ▶ to hear one; use headphones, or it is recorded too). The whole recording can be played once you stop.

The sound is saved in the program's recordings folder as it comes in. While listening, other transcription waits so this one keeps up.

Highlighted words are the ones the model was unsure about. Click any word to hear it and edit it; Shift+click selects several words. The player above plays the whole recording and highlights each word as it is spoken; click a time to play from that line.
